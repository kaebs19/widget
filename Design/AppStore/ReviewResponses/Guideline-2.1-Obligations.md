Thank you for your question. No actual money is deducted from users by the app.

The “خصم الالتزامات قبل الموعد” option shown in your screenshot is an optional budgeting calculation, not a payment or bank deduction. Users manually enter a budget amount and their planned obligations, such as rent or bills. When this option is enabled, the app subtracts the manually entered obligations from the entered budget for calculation purposes, then divides the remainder by the days until the selected payday to estimate a daily spending allowance.

For example, an entered budget of SAR 3,000, planned obligations of SAR 1,000, and 20 remaining days would produce an estimated daily allowance of SAR 100: (3,000 − 1,000) ÷ 20.

The app does not connect to bank accounts, collect banking credentials, initiate payments, transfer funds, or automatically pay obligations. The amounts are manually entered records stored locally on the user's device. The quick expense buttons only update these local budgeting records; they do not charge the user. Users can disable the obligations calculation at any time.

We understand that the Arabic word for “deduct” may have caused confusion. It refers only to subtracting a number within the budgeting calculation, not deducting real money.
